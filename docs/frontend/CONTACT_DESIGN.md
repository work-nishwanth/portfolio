# Contact design

The closing section is a correspondence-inspired composition: oversized Anton typography with a warm Instrument Serif greeting, a folded letter linking to email, a readable address with a separate copy action, and understated location/social rows. It reuses existing theme tokens and fonts.

The letter straightens and lifts on hover/focus; the arrow moves slightly toward its destination. Reduced motion removes these transitions. Both letter and visible address open the same mailto destination. Clipboard feedback is announced through a status region; denied access leaves the visible email selectable.

Keep the desktop slide fully readable at 620px height. At 900px width and below the composition stacks in reading order and uses natural content height. Preserve the homepage navigation, projects route, and case-study behavior.
